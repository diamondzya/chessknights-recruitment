Membership Requirements

Are You Ready to Join?

If you meet the following requirements, you're one step closer to becoming a ChessKnight.

Enrolled Student

Must be currently enrolled at STI Ortigas–Cainta.

Interest in Leadership

Willing to learn, collaborate, and participate in organizational activities.

Good Character

Demonstrates respect, responsibility, and professionalism inside and outside the classroom.

Commitment

Ready to actively support ChessKnights programs, projects, and events.

Important Reminder

Meeting the minimum requirements does not automatically guarantee membership. Applicants may undergo screening and an interview as part of the recruitment process.
